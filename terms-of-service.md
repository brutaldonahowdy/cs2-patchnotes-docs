## What the app does

The app is a Reddit Developer Platform (Devvit) app. When a moderator installs it on a subreddit, the app checks Steam for new Counter-Strike 2 (CS2) announcements once a minute and posts each new announcement to that subreddit:

- A patch note is posted as a link to the Steam news page. The patch notes are posted as the first comment.
- Other announcements are posted as links to the Steam news page.
- Posts and comments are made by the app's Reddit account.

Moderators of a subreddit where the app is installed can also start a check manually from the subreddit's moderator menu, and can choose the post flair in the app settings.
