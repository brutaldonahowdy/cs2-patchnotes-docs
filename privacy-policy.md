# CS2 Update Announcer: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

This policy describes the data that the CS2 Update Announcer app (the "app", Reddit app name `cs2-patchnotes`), operated by u/brutaldonahowdy (the "operator"), uses, stores and shares.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store or share personal data about Reddit users. It reads public announcements from Steam and posts them to the subreddits where it is installed.

## Data the app reads

- Public CS2 announcements from Steam: the announcement and event IDs, title, publication time and text. The app requests them from `store.steampowered.com`.
- The post flair templates of the subreddit where it is installed, to find the flair to apply to its posts.
- The name of the subreddit where it is installed.
- The app's own settings for that subreddit (the post flair text).

When a moderator uses the app's menu item, Reddit passes the request to the app with information about the request, which can include the moderator's account. The app does not record or store this information.

## Data the app stores

The app stores the following data in the storage that the Reddit Developer Platform provides for each installation:

- The IDs of Steam announcements that the app has processed, each with the time it was processed.
- The time of the app's last post.
- A marker that shows that the installation is set up, and a lock that expires after one minute.

None of this data is about Reddit users. Each subreddit's data is separate. The operator does not copy this data to any other system.

The app writes operational logs, for example the titles of Steam announcements and the IDs of the posts it makes. The Reddit Developer Platform keeps these logs for a limited period (currently up to 7 days). The logs contain no personal data, other than the subreddit name and the IDs of the app's own posts.

## Data the app shares

The app sends requests to Steam (`store.steampowered.com`, operated by Valve Corporation) to read public announcements. These requests contain no data about Reddit users or subreddits. They come from Reddit's servers, so Steam does not receive the IP address of any Reddit user.

The app posts the announcements to Reddit. These posts and comments are public Reddit content.

The app does not sell data, and does not share data with any other party.

## Reddit

The app runs on the Reddit Developer Platform. Reddit's own processing of data, for example in its logs and storage, follows the Reddit Privacy Policy.

## Retention and deletion

Stored data stays for as long as the app is installed on the subreddit. When a moderator removes the app from a subreddit, Reddit can delete that installation's stored data. To ask for deletion of data, contact the operator (see below).

## Changes to this policy

The operator can change this policy. The current version is at this page, with its effective date at the top.
